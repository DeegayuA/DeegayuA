## About

**Research & Development Engineer at [Alta Vision PLC](https://altavision.lk)**, where I engineer the commercial Solar SCADA and mini-grid management platform running across utility-scale solar farms, plus the internal tooling and AI-assisted systems around it.

**Visiting Lecturer** at Gampaha Wickramarachchi University of Indigenous Medicine, teaching Web Application Development, Mobile App Development and Artificial Intelligence.

Reading for a **Master of Data Science & AI** at the University of Moratuwa and a **Master of Information Technology** at the University of Kelaniya. Background in physics and electronics, so I am equally at home writing ESP32 firmware, training a YOLO model, or shipping a Next.js app.
